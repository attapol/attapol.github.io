Thai NLP

Selected contributions to Thai natural language processing.

AttaCut

A fast and accurate neural Thai word tokenizer. The model comprises a three-layer dilated CNN over character and syllable features, running at least 5.6x faster and outperforming prior state-of-the-art systems on selected domains.

GitHub Paper (arXiv)

PhayaThaiBERT POS tagger

A UPOS Part-of-Speech tagging model, based on fine-tuned PhayaThaiBERT and trained on the Thai Universal Dependency Treebank (see ThaiUD below). Reaches 0.91 accuracy and 0.81 macro F1 on the test set. Best used with pre-segmented words, e.g. from AttaCut.

Hugging Face

ThaiUD

TACL 2025

A Thai Universal Dependency (UD) treebank with 3,627 trees sampled from the Thai National Corpus and Thai Wikipedia, annotated with 15 UPOS tags and 35 dependency relations. The release includes benchmark transformer parsers pre-trained on TUD and Thai-PUD, and the test-set predictions and confusion matrices of 92 models.

GitHub Universal Dependencies Paper (TACL)

ThaiCoref

A large Thai coreference resolution dataset, comprising 777,271 tokens, 44,082 mentions, and 10,429 entities across university essays, newspapers, speeches, and Wikipedia articles. The annotation scheme builds upon OntoNotes with adjustments for Thai-specific features. The best baseline, a multilingual encoder with cross-lingual transfer, reaches 0.68 F1.

GitHub Paper (LRE)

Thai Discourse Treebank

TACL 2024

The first large Thai corpus with explicit discourse relations à la Penn Discourse Treebank 3, comprising 10,602 sentences from 384 documents, about 18,000 connective instances and 33 relations. The release includes a list of 148 potentially polysemous connectives with 340 form-sense pairs. Baselines reach 0.96 F1 for connective identification and 0.46 for sense classification.

GitHub Paper (TACL)

SCB-MT-EN-TH-2020

An English-Thai parallel corpus of 1,001,752 segment pairs from news, Wikipedia, SMS, task-based dialogs, web crawls, and government documents, released with the alignment and filtering pipeline. Transformer models trained on this corpus perform comparably to the Google Translation API (as of May 2020), and outperform it in both directions when Open Parallel Corpus data is added.

JAI-1

A Thai-centric language model with 75B parameters, constructed by systematically integrating Thai-language knowledge to a smaller, high-performing English model. Pre-training involved 1.5T tokens, over 300B of which are Thai, while post-training utilized more than 600K instruction-based examples. The model outperforms Typhoon2-70B on IFEval-TH, MT-Bench-TH and JAI-Hall-Bench benchmarks. The open-source OpenJAI-v1.0 release is a separate 14B model built on Qwen3-14B (Apache-2.0).

JAI-TTS

A state-of-the-art autoregressive Thai voice cloning text-to-speech model built on VoxCPM. Trained on a Thai-centric speech corpus of 10,000 hours, the model is able to read raw text with numerals and Thai–English code-switching without additional text normalization steps. Reaches a 1.94% CER on short utterances, slightly surpassing the human ground truth of 1.98%, and matches humans on long utterances. Wins 283 of 400 pairwise comparisons against commercial flagships (59 ties, 58 losses) in human judgment evaluations.

Hugging Face Paper (arXiv)
